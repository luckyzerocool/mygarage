Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Your data stays with you

MY GARAGE stores your vehicles, photos, documents, details and history only on your device. There are no servers: the app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Your optional profile

You can add your name, a profile photo, driving licence details and an emergency contact. They are optional, stored only on your device (and in backups you choose to make), never uploaded, and the licence number and photos are locked with Face ID or your passcode.

No account, no tracking, no ads

You never need an account. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to take photos of your vehicles and to scan registration certificates, insurance policies and other documents. Photos you take stay in the app.
  • Photos: only the pictures you choose are copied into the app.
  • Face ID: only to lock details and files; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications: only for the reminders you set.

Backups

If you choose a backup folder, for example in iCloud Drive, the app copies your garage into it. That folder is kept by Apple or the provider you chose, under their privacy policy.

Sharing

Information leaves the app only when you share it yourself, for example a vehicle dossier or a document.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Backups you made stay in the folder you chose until you delete them.

Children

MY GARAGE is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
