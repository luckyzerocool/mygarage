Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone or iPad. MY GARAGE has no account and no servers.

How do I back up?
Settings → Backup Folder → choose a folder in iCloud Drive. The app then backs up every day, and you can restore at any time.

How do I move my garage to a new iPhone or my iPad?
Back up on the old device, then on the new one open Settings → Restore from Backup and choose the newest backup.

Reminders don't arrive.
Allow notifications for MY GARAGE in the iPhone Settings, and enter the dates in a vehicle's Details.

The widget shows no photo.
Long-press the widget → Edit Widget → choose a vehicle that has photos.
